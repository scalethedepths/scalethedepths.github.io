What Is Scale the Depths?

Scale the Depths is a cozy pixel-styled fishing simulation game developed by Canadian indie studio Glass Gecko Games. You play as a tiny robot fishmonger tasked with catching fish, scaling them perfectly, and serving them to an increasingly bizarre lineup of underwater customers.

Originally created for the 2024 GMTK Game Jam, it quickly went viral thanks to its addictive core loop, accumulating over 210,000 demo players and a 97% positive rating. The full version launches on May 28, 2026 on Steam.

The Core Gameplay Loop

Simple concept, addictive depth — that's the magic of Scale the Depths

1
🎣

Cast Your Line

Drop your fishing hook into the water and guide it left and right to slam into swimming fish. Some fish are quick, others are armored — all require careful steering to catch.

2
✂️

Scale the Fish

Carry your catch to the scaling station. Use precise mouse drags to strip fish of their shiny scales. Go too fast and you risk damaging the catch — find the right rhythm!

3
🍽️

Serve Your Customers

Feed the scaled fish to strange aquatic customers who pay based on their satisfaction. Each customer has unique preferences — match the right fish to earn maximum coins.

4
⚡

Upgrade Your Gear

Spend your hard-earned coins on sharper knives, longer fishing lines, better hooks, multi-catch upgrades, and new boats.

5
🌊

Go Deeper

With better equipment, explore deeper waters filled with rarer, more valuable, and more bizarre fish species. Each depth layer brings new challenges and secrets.

6
🔁

Repeat

The loop tightens with each cycle. You find yourself thinking &ldquo;just one more run&rdquo; as the satisfaction builds and the depths call you back.

The Four Worlds

Four distinct locations inspired by real-world places, each with unique environments, exclusive fish, and hidden secrets

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

Loch Ness

The first and most iconic location. The misty Scottish loch is home to Nessie herself — one of your most demanding (and rewarding) customers. The waters here are relatively shallow but filled with personality. Look for messages in bottles and hidden paths along the loch floor.

🏝️

Outer Banks

The barrier islands off the coast of North Carolina bring warmer waters and a different ecosystem of fish. This location introduces more complex scaling challenges and customers with stronger opinions about how their fish should be prepared.

🌊

Point Nemo

The oceanic pole of inaccessibility — the most remote point in the ocean. The waters are deep, dark, and filled with creatures that shouldn&apos;t exist. The game shifts from cozy fishing into something more mysterious and unsettling. The lore deepens here.

❓

The Unknown

The final location pushes beyond the boundaries of normal fishing. The deeper you go, the further from reality you drift. Ancient creatures, forgotten technology, and the truth behind why a tiny robot was built to scale fish in the first place...

Tips & Strategies

Whether you're playing the browser version or waiting for the full Steam release, these tips will help you get started

🎯
#1

Master the Scaling Rhythm

Don't rush — smooth, even mouse drags produce the best results. Think of it like a rhythm game: find the tempo that works for each fish type and stick with it. Damaged scales mean less money.

🔍
#2

Learn Customer Preferences Early

Each customer has favorite fish types. Serving the right fish to the right customer earns significantly more coins. Pay attention to their reactions and dialogue cues.

📏
#3

Prioritize Line Length Upgrades

In the early game, upgrading your fishing line length gives you access to deeper fish worth more money. This creates a positive feedback loop: deeper fish → more money → better gear → even deeper fish.

📜
#4

Don't Ignore the Lore

Messages in bottles and hidden notes aren't just flavor text — they contain hints about secret areas and optimal fishing spots. Players who follow the lore often find rare fish locations others miss.

💎
#5

Save Your Best Fish for Special Customers

It's tempting to sell every fish as fast as possible, but holding onto rare catches for mythical customers like Nessie pays off enormously. Legendary customers give the biggest payouts — but only if you serve their favorites.

From Game Jam to Steam Hit

The story behind Scale the Depths is almost as interesting as the game itself

August 2024

GMTK Game Jam Birth

Seven developers created Scale the Depths for the 2024 GMTK Game Jam. The jam's theme inspired the core "scaling" mechanic — the tactile satisfaction of stripping fish scales with a mouse drag. The prototype was an instant hit on itch.io.

2024–2026

The Demo Phenomenon

210,000+ demo players, 97% positive rating, 115,000+ wishlists, millions of YouTube views. A tiny game jam project became one of Steam's most anticipated indie releases.

2025–2026

The Knockoff Problem

The game's popularity spawned a wave of unauthorized clones, particularly on mobile platforms. The developers publicly acknowledged: "In case you've seen 'similar' mobile ads floating around... this is the original 😉"

May 28, 2026

Full Steam Release

Partnering with publishers Pretty Soon and Phoenix Games, the complete version brings four explorable worlds, deeper progression, hidden secrets, customization, collectibles, and lore that goes far beyond what anyone expects from a game about preparing fish.
